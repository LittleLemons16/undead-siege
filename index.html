WAVE SURVIVAL HORROR

UNDEAD SIEGE

7 MAPS · 38 GUNS · 4 DIFFICULTIES · CO-OP · DAILY CHALLENGE

Survive as many rounds as you can. Earn points for hits and kills, open doors, buy weapons off the walls, gamble on the Mystery Crate and grab perks.

How to play

WASD move · Mouse aim & fire · R reload · E buy / use / stairs · P pause · U shop (between rounds) · G grenade · Shift sprint · Q or 1/2/3 switch weapon

Gamepad: sticks move/aim · RT fire · A use · X reload · Y swap · B grenade · LB sprint
Touch: left side moves, right side aims & fires
